Reliable & secure — on your machine

Aspera Hub is a desktop app. Your WhatsApp and Zoho logins stay under your user profile. There is no Hub cloud account and no Aspera server in the middle.

  • SHA-256 verified updatesGitHub Releases only. A corrupted or tampered download is refused before install.
  • Guest sandboxWeb apps run with contextIsolation and sandbox — no Node in the page.
  • Password lockOptional whole-workspace lock; auto-lock on idle or sleep. Shortcut: Ctrl+Shift+L.
  • Isolated partitionsEach profile is its own Electron persist session — no cookie bleed.
  • Single instanceOne Hub window per profile data dir — no accidental double sessions.
  • Open source MITRead it, modify it, share it. Free forever — no subscription, no ads.

Good to know

Is it really free?
Yes — free, forever. No subscription, no trial wall, no ads. Open source under the MIT license. 100% free for life.
Which Linux distros are supported?
Debian, Ubuntu and Linux Mint (amd64). Other Debian-based distributions usually work with the same .deb. Tuned especially for Mint Cinnamon and XFCE fleets.
How is it different from Rambox or a browser?
The warm/cold design keeps your daily apps instant while letting the rest sleep — typically lighter on RAM — and it's free and Linux-first. You also get a real desktop shell: badges, tray, mute, focus, lock, downloads, Hub pins, Forward, and isolated logins.
Where do my logins live?
On your machine under ~/.config/Aspera Dock. Clearing a session in Hub signs that app out locally. There is no Hub cloud account.
How many apps can stay warm?
By default 5 warm apps stay fully loaded. You can raise the cap to 7 in Settings. Everything else loads on click and can hibernate after idle. Max 10 apps in the dock.
Does Aspera AI cost money?
Hub never charges. AI is optional and bring-your-own-key (Gemini, Sarvam, Grok, DeepSeek, SambaNova, OpenRouter, or Anthropic). Many providers offer free tiers. Keys stay on your machine.
Can I add any website?
Today Hub uses the Aspera catalog only (WhatsApp, Arattai, Gmail, Zoho Mail/CRM/Books/One). You can run multiple copies of the same app with separate profiles. The catalog will grow over time.

Honest limits

  • Primary package is amd64 .deb for Debian-family desktops.
  • Forward and Hub pins are for WhatsApp ↔ Arattai.
  • Aspera AI skills apply to WhatsApp, Arattai, Gmail, and Zoho Mail.
  • Hardware acceleration defaults off on Linux for Mint stability (you can enable it).
